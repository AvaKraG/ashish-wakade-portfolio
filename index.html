02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Good design starts
with understanding.

I’m Ashish Wakade, a UI/UX designer interested in understanding how people work, uncovering the real problems behind a process and turning them into clear digital experiences.

I have worked on government digital service projects, collaborating with government employees to understand service requirements and existing offline processes. This involved exploring how applicants access services and schemes, mapping applicant journeys and internal workflows, and translating those findings into user journeys, workflows and UI/UX designs.

That experience shaped the way I approach design: start by understanding the people and the process, then make the experience simpler and more usable. I’ve also completed the Google UX Design Certificate and continue to develop my practice through independent case studies.

CASE STUDY 01 · CONCEPT PROJECT

Home Tutor

A two-sided marketplace concept designed to help parents find suitable home tutors with less friction and more confidence.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Exploring how tutor discovery, search and comparison could become clearer for parents navigating a crowded marketplace.

DESIGN FOCUS

Information architecture, discoverability, search experience, trust signals and the parent-to-tutor connection journey.

CASE STUDY 02 · CONCEPT PROJECT

Clean City

A civic reporting app concept that helps residents report neighbourhood waste and track progress towards resolution.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Exploring how a simple reporting journey can connect citizens with local civic teams and make follow-through visible.

DESIGN FOCUS

Photo-based reporting, location capture, report status, updates and a transparent resolution journey.

CASE STUDY 03 · CONCEPT PROJECT

Medication Manager

A medication management concept for keeping prescriptions, daily schedules, reminders and refills organised.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Exploring how people can manage recurring medication routines without having to remember every detail themselves.

DESIGN FOCUS

Medication lists, schedules, reminders, refill planning and clear, accessible daily information.
